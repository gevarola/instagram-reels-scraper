## 1. Fortune Teller ("The Predictor")

**Goal:** Paint an optimistic or dramatic future scenario. Position yourself as someone who sees what's coming.

**Psychology:** Taps into FOMO and the desire to be prepared. Viewers stay because they want to know if the prediction is credible.

**Template structures:**
- "This [new thing] is gonna completely change the way [people] do [task]. Here's why..."
- "We're witnessing the biggest shift in [industry] history."
- "In 12 months, [current norm] won't exist anymore."
- "[Small thing happening now] is about to become the most important trend in [space]."

**Who should use:** Best for building trust as someone who knows "what's next." Positions you as a visionary.

**When to use:** When something new drops in your space or an industry shift is happening.

**DeFi examples:**
- "This one protocol update is about to make liquidity mining obsolete."
- "El swap de 200ms que va a hacer que los CEX se queden sin clientes."
- "Aave just quietly released something that changes lending forever."

**AI examples:**
- "This free tool just made $50K marketing agencies worthless."
- "Lo que OpenAI acaba de hacer va a matar a 90% de las agencias de contenido."

## 2. Experimenter ("The Tester")

**Goal:** Show a process, experiment, or hack you ran. Explain how you solved a common pain point.

**Psychology:** Curiosity through vicarious experience. Viewers live through your experiment. The promise of discovery keeps them watching.

**Template structures:**
- "I tried [X] for [time period] — here's what actually happened."
- "I put [amount] into [thing] and tracked everything for [duration]."
- "What happens when you [unexpected action]? I found out."
- "I tested every [tool/method] so you don't have to."

**Who should use:** Great for peer-to-peer positioning. You're a fellow explorer, not a guru.

**When to use:** When you're one of the first to try something new, or you've run a real test.

**DeFi examples:**
- "Metí $5,000 en 5 protocolos DeFi por 30 días — estos son mis rendimientos REALES."
- "I provided liquidity on Uniswap v4 with hooks for a week. Here's the data."

**AI examples:**
- "Generé 30 videos con Veo 3 en una semana — esto es lo que aprendí."
- "I replaced my entire content team with AI for 14 days."

## 3. Teacher ("The Expert")

**Goal:** Provide solutions or explain methods. Build credibility as the go-to authority.

**Psychology:** Satisfies the desire to learn something useful, quickly. Viewers stay for practical value.

**Template structures:**
- "Here's how to [achieve result] in [short timeframe]."
- "The [number] [tactics/methods] you should steal from [successful entity]."
- "Stop doing [common mistake]. Do this instead."
- "La forma más inteligente de [lograr resultado] sin [dolor común]."

**Who should use:** Best for establishing authority. Positions you as the expert.

**When to use:** When you have a proven method or insight that solves a real problem.

**DeFi examples:**
- "3 estrategias de yield farming que los whales no quieren que conozcas."
- "How to bridge assets to L2s without losing money on gas."

**AI examples:**
- "El workflow exacto para crear 6 piezas de contenido de 1 sola idea."
- "How I write viral scripts in 10 minutes with Claude."

## 4. Magician ("The Stunner")

**Goal:** Use visually or audibly striking elements to grab attention INSTANTLY. Pure sensory impact.

**Psychology:** The "stun gun" effect. Something unexpected hijacks the viewer's attention before their rational brain can decide to scroll.

**Template structures:**
- Quick cuts showing dramatic transformation
- Bold visual paired with "Check this out!" or "Watch this."
- Rhythmic sound + surprising visual montage
- Before/after reveal in the first 2 seconds

**Who should use:** Best combined with other archetypes. Rarely works alone for educational content.

**When to use:** When you have striking visuals — demos, reveals, transformations.

**DeFi examples:**
- Screen recording of a swap completing in 200ms with a dramatic sound hit
- Quick cuts: wallet balance → transaction → new balance, all in 3 seconds

**AI examples:**
- Side-by-side: blank screen → AI-generated video in 5 seconds
- Quick montage of 10 AI tools generating outputs simultaneously

## 5. Investigator ("The Detective")

**Goal:** Reveal secrets, hidden information, or lesser-known insights. Make viewers feel they're getting insider knowledge.

**Psychology:** Taps into curiosity and the desire for privileged information. Creates in-group feeling.

**Template structures:**
- "The hidden [thing] about [topic] that nobody tells you about."
- "I spent [time] investigating [entity] — what I found is disturbing."
- "There's a reason [popular company/protocol] doesn't want you to know this."
- "El dato que [entidad] no quiere que veas."

**Who should use:** Best for deep dives, research-heavy content, and contrarian analysis.

**When to use:** When you've found something through research that isn't common knowledge.

**DeFi examples:**
- "The hidden fee structure in this 'zero-fee' DEX that's costing you thousands."
- "Lo que realmente pasa con tus fondos cuando haces stake en [protocolo]."

**AI examples:**
- "What happens to your data when you use free AI tools — I traced it."
- "El costo real de las APIs de AI que nadie te dice."

## 6. Contrarian ("The Challenger")

**Goal:** Challenge conventional wisdom. Say the opposite of what everyone believes.

**Psychology:** Cognitive dissonance. When someone challenges what you believe, you MUST watch to defend your position or update your worldview.

**Template structures:**
- "[Popular thing] is completely overrated — here's why."
- "Everyone's wrong about [topic]. The data proves it."
- "Stop [popular advice]. It's killing your [results]."
- "[Consensus opinion] es completamente falso — te explico por qué."

**Who should use:** Best for establishing thought leadership. Shows you think independently.

**When to use:** When you genuinely have evidence against popular opinion. Never be contrarian just for engagement — have the receipts.

**DeFi examples:**
- "Everyone says DeFi is the future of finance — they're wrong, and here's why that's actually bullish."
- "Uniswap v4 hooks are NOT what you think they are."

**AI examples:**
- "AI is NOT replacing developers — it's doing something far more interesting."
- "El problema con usar AI para todo tu contenido que nadie quiere admitir."

---

## 7. Combining Archetypes

The strongest hooks often combine 2 archetypes:

- **Fortune Teller + Contrarian:** "Everyone thinks [X] is the future. They're about to be proven wrong."
- **Investigator + Experimenter:** "I spent 30 days investigating [X] and what I found changes everything."
- **Teacher + Magician:** [Visual stun] + "Here's exactly how you can do this too."

## Hook Validation Checklist

Before publishing, run every hook through this checklist:

- [ ] Does it create a specific curiosity gap? (not vague)
- [ ] Can you identify which archetype it uses?
- [ ] Are all 4 components aligned? (visual, spoken, text, audio)
- [ ] Would YOU stop scrolling for this?
- [ ] Is it specific enough? (numbers, names, timeframes > vague claims)
- [ ] Does it promise a payoff the content actually delivers?
- [ ] Is it under 10 words for the text overlay?
