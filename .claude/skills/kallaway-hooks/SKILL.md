# Hooks — Kallaway Method

## Core Philosophy

Every hook has one job: get the viewer to opt in and keep watching. To do that, deliver two things simultaneously:
1. **Topic clarity** — the viewer knows exactly what this is about in the first 1-2 seconds
2. **On-target curiosity** — they believe the topic is for them and want to know what comes next

The mechanism: a **curiosity loop** — a psychological gap between what the viewer knows and what you tease. The fuel: **contrast** — the distance between the viewer's current belief (A) and the alternative you introduce (B). The bigger the contrast, the deeper the hook.

The underlying psychology: viewers pay with time. They only do it if they believe the video will unlock one of the four base desires — money, time, health, or status. The most potent driver is **fear** (FOMO) — the art is making the viewer feel it without stating it directly.

**Hooks are 80% of the 80/20 in content.** Fix hooks first. Everything else is secondary.

## Key Frameworks

Full templates and detailed breakdowns are in `references/hooks-reference.md`.

### 1. The Three-Step Hook Formula
The base formula for all hooks. Works for every niche, every format.

- **Context Lean** — Give topic clarity + start the curiosity loop in 1-2 sentences. Common ground, benefit, pain point, or mind-blowing fact.
- **Scroll Stop Interjection** — One line that stuns. Use a contrast pivot word: "but," "however," "yet." This is the setup, not the payoff.
- **Contrarian Snapback** — Snap in the opposite direction. Still on-topic. The bigger the shock, the better. This is the haymaker.

### 2. The Four Hook Components (Must Align)
The difference between 500 views and 500,000 views is alignment between all four:
- **Visual hook** — the key visual on screen (processed first, most important)
- **Spoken hook** — what you say verbally
- **Text hook** — 3-5 word overlay on screen (not captions)
- **Audio hook** — music, sound effect, or intentional silence

Viewer processing order is visual → audio → visual. All four must point in the same direction. Any misalignment causes comprehension loss and immediate churn.

**Start with the visual.** Identify the most compelling thing you can show in seconds 0-3 before writing any spoken hook.

### 3. The Six Hook Archetypes
Any topic can use any of the six. The key visual determines which creates the most contrast.

| # | Archetype | Core Contrast | Best For |
|---|-----------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | **Fortune Teller** | Present vs. future | Breaking news, product launches, predictions |
| 2 | **Experimenter** | Old method vs. new discovery | Product demos, tests, real results |
| 3 | **Teacher** | Failing vs. the fix | Tutorials, frameworks, authority building |
| 4 | **Magician** | Pattern interrupt | Stacks with any archetype, visual stunts |
| 5 | **Investigator** | What you don't know vs. what I found | Research, exposés, niche authority |
| 6 | **Contrarian** | Common belief vs. your actual take | Thought leadership, debate, hot takes |

See `references/hooks-reference.md` for per-archetype templates, examples, and tactical steps.

### 4. The Desire-Based Hook (God-Tier for Conversions)
Best hook format for turning views into leads and sales. Signal two things in the first 1-2 lines:
1. A **relatable character** the viewer sees themselves in
2. Their **dream outcome** — what the character achieved

Five templates: About Me (backward) / If I (forward hypothetical) / To You (viewer as subject) / Can You? (question) / He/She Just Did (third-party).

**Critical rule:** The character must have no conditions or unfair advantages the viewer cannot replicate.

### 5. The Four Hook Mistakes (Diagnostic)
Every bad hook is making at least one of these:
- **Delay** — topic context arrives too late. Fix: topic clarity in line one.
- **Confusion** — unclear sentence structure. Fix: sixth-grade reading level, active voice.
- **Irrelevance** — viewer can't see themselves in it. Fix: use "you/your" not "I/me," frame around a pain point.
- **Disinterest** — clear but not curious. Fix: build contrast (stated or implied) in the hook.

### 6. Single Subject / Single Question Rule
One hook = one topic + one question planted in every viewer's mind. If 50,000 people watch, all 50,000 should be asking the same question. If you cannot name the single question your hook plants, rewrite it.

## Process (7-Step Hook Writing Checklist)

Apply this every time.

1. **Define the single subject.** What is this video actually about? Be specific before writing anything.
2. **Decide the single question.** What emotion-inducing or shock-driven question should pop in the viewer's mind? It must lead perfectly into the next part of the script.
3. **Identify the key visual.** What is the most compelling thing you can show in seconds 0-3? Know this before writing the spoken hook.
4. **Pick the archetype.** Given the key visual, which of the six creates the most contrast for this topic?
5. **Write the spoken hook.** 1-3 sentences. Fifth-grade vocabulary. Active voice. Short staccato sentences. No filler.
6. **Run the gut check.** Does this hook unmistakably reference one subject? Does it unmistakably pop one single question? If no to either — rewrite.
7. **Execute the full alignment.** Visual + text overlay + spoken + audio all point the same direction. Watch it back. Answer: Am I clear on the subject? Am I clear on the question? Are all components aligned? Am I curious enough to continue?

## Anti-Patterns

- **Never** open with "Hey guys, so today..." — zero context, zero curiosity, immediate churn.
- **Never** delay the topic beyond the first two sentences — the retention cliff is steepest in the first 2 seconds.
- **Never** use passive voice or long complex sentences in a hook — raises cognitive load, kills comprehension.
- **Never** use "I/me" framing when "you/your" works — forces the viewer to check if they see themselves in you.
- **Never** add conditions the viewer can't replicate ("This $1,200 device changed my life") — breaks desire-based hooks.
- **Never** let visual, text, and spoken hooks point in different directions — misalignment causes a comprehension freeze.
- **Never** choose the hook archetype before knowing your key visual — you may have no visual to execute on.
- **Never** benchmark hooks against the biggest creators — their brand carries weak hooks. Study small-to-mid creators with breakout videos.
- **Never** lead directly with the desire ("make more money") — trips the BS detector. Go one standard deviation away — target the proxy desire.
- **Never** hammer fear for 90 seconds without teasing the solve early — the viewers who feel the pain most will churn before the payoff.
- **Never** write hooks after the script — write the hook first, build the video around it.
- **Never** use generic curiosity phrases ("you won't believe this") without specificity — the subject must be identifiable.

## Output Format

When generating hooks, always produce this exact format. Generate at least 3 variations using different archetypes.

```
HOOK [N] — [ARCHETYPE NAME]
SPOKEN HOOK: "[exact words verbatim]"
TEXT OVERLAY: "[3-5 words max]"
VISUAL DIRECTION: [what to show in seconds 0-3]
AUDIO NOTE: [music hit / sound effect / silence]
CURIOSITY LOOP: [the single question this plants in the viewer's mind]
WHY IT WORKS: [1 sentence on the contrast mechanism]
```

After all variations, add:

```
RECOMMENDED: Hook [N] — [reason why this archetype has the strongest contrast for this specific topic/visual]
```

## Application to DeFi / AI / LATAM

### Niche Priority Guide

For **DeFi/crypto** content, prioritize:
- **Investigator** — hidden risks, hidden opportunities, protocol deep dives
- **Contrarian** — challenge the consensus narrative ("everyone loves X — they're wrong")
- **Fortune Teller** — fast-moving market, predictions create outsized curiosity
- **Experimenter** — real money, real results, real timeframes (irresistible in crypto)

For **AI/tech** content, prioritize:
- **Experimenter** — live demos of tools are the strongest visual hook available
- **Fortune Teller** — industry-shift framing ("this changes everything for [profession]")
- **Teacher** — workflows and frameworks build authority fast in a crowded space

For **LATAM/Spanish** content: hooks must feel conversational and direct. Never translate literally from English. Adapt the rhythm and energy to Latin American Spanish — shorter sentences, more direct address, colloquial register.

### English Hook Templates — DeFi/AI

**Fortune Teller — DeFi**
```
This protocol is about to make every centralized exchange obsolete.
Not in 5 years. Right now.
```

**Experimenter — DeFi**
```
I put $10,000 into this unknown DeFi protocol for 30 days.
Here are my actual returns — and the one thing I didn't expect.
```

**Investigator — DeFi**
```
There's a hidden risk in this "safe" yield protocol that no one is talking about.
I spent two weeks digging into the contracts. Here's what I found.
```

**Contrarian — DeFi**
```
Everyone is calling this the Uniswap killer. They're completely wrong.
And if you don't understand why, you're going to lose money.
```

**Desire-Based (To You) — AI**
```
If you want to get better results from AI tools, this one workflow change
will cut your prompt time in half.
```

**Three-Step Formula — AI**
```
[Context Lean] This new AI agent can control your entire browser.
[Scroll Stop] But the feature nobody is talking about isn't the automation.
[Contrarian Snapback] It's the one thing it refuses to do — and that's the whole point.
```
