# Content System — Kallaway Method

## Core Philosophy

**Be a scientist, not an artist.** Form a hypothesis, test it, read data, adjust, repeat. Intuition is a liability early. Data is the only signal that matters.

**De-risk the rep.** Every video must simultaneously: (1) find the right viewers, (2) resonate with them, (3) convert some to action. Design the system around hitting all three, every rep.

**Ideas above everything.** Spend the most time on ideas and the least time on editing. Millions of unscripted, unedited bedroom videos go viral daily — because the idea was a banger.

**Volume is a forcing function.** If you post high volume and get better rep-to-rep, you cannot lose. Volume eliminates overthinking and accelerates skill acquisition faster than any other method.

**Distribution > product in 2025+.** Attention is the biggest wave. Content is the vehicle. Own it now.

## Key Frameworks

### 1. The Bad Sampling Model (Diagnose First)

The algorithm shows every new video to 100–200 people. Weak sample data = algorithm kills the video. Three causes:
1. Fake followers — bots don't watch. Fix: start fresh.
2. Wrong followers — mismatched audience. Fix: recalibrate or restart.
3. Bad video — most common. Fix: use the frameworks below.

### 2. No Man's Land (Pick a Game)

Content only works at extremes:
- **Entertainment:** Shock, novelty, maximum TAM. Compete with Netflix and Mr. Beast.
- **Education:** Tactically useful. Viewer can apply the learning today. Compete with ChatGPT and Google.

Edutainment = losing both games. Pick one lane per channel. "If you're in the middle, you will lose."

### 3. The Idea Trinity (Topic + Premise + Hook)

Every video = three components:
- **Topic:** One-line concept.
- **Premise (Angle):** The specific lens, approach, or take that makes this video interesting.
- **Hook:** Visual + spoken + text on screen + audio — all four aligned — executed in the first 3–5 seconds.

For education: the hook must target the viewer's pain point with crystal clarity.
For entertainment: the hook must trigger shock + curiosity + maximum TAM relatability.

### 4. The Idea Mining System (Sandcastles + Outlier Extraction)

Run this 1 hour/week. Do not skip.

1. Build a watch list of 15–20 top accounts in your niche on Sandcastles.ai.
2. Filter: last 30–60 days, engagement rate ≥ 0.5%, sort by outlier score or views.
3. Watch 25–50 validated winner videos per session.
4. Extract 7 variables per video: Topic, Angle, Hook, Story Format, Visual Format, Visuals, Audio.
5. Build a superset of Lego bricks per variable.
6. Stack bricks to build your version. Apply Virgil Abloh's 3% rule: change enough to make it yours.

This is "four times distilled" ideation: raw feed → newsletters → niche creators → Sandcastles watch list → outlier filter = pure signal, zero noise.

### 5. The Four Buckets of Every Video

Analyze and improve any video across four dimensions:
1. **Idea/Premise** — angle, topic choice, opportunity space.
2. **Hook Structure** — what you say and show in the first 3–5 seconds.
3. **Story Structure** — body flow, logic line, rehooks.
4. **Visual Structure** — B-roll, motion, editing clarity.

Find the weakest bucket. Fix only that. Kallaway's Toyota Woven City fix: same idea as Rowan's 40K-view video → 2.2M views (55x) by tightening the hook, adding a missing visual layer, and switching to breakdown structure with rehooks.

### 6. The Content Seasons Model

Measure progress in reps, not calendar time:
- **Season 1 (Reps 1–3):** Post no matter what. Do not evaluate metrics.
- **Season 2 (Reps 4–50):** Focus exclusively on input skills. Metrics will lag — this is expected. "Skill acquisition in content is a one-player game with a 100% win rate. As long as you keep going."
- **Season 3 (Reps 50–200):** Skills start earning metrics. Now check the scoreboard.

### 7. Quantity vs. Quality Progression

Follow this sequence exactly:

1. **Get to 7/10 quality.** Post until you can produce a video you believe is 7th-best out of 10 on that topic. Below 7/10 = almost certain loss.
2. **Max volume at 7/10.** Short form: 5/week. YouTube: 1/week. Do not chase 8/10 yet. Volume will incidentally raise quality through reps.
3. **Push to 9/10** while maintaining volume targets.
4. **Double volume.** Short form: 10/week. YouTube: 2/week at 9/10 quality. This is world-class.

### 8. Methodical Expansion (One Platform First)

1. Pick one platform + one content format + one topic cluster.
2. Build a system that runs 30 consecutive days without breaking.
3. Hire out every non-delegatable task so quality and quantity hold constant.
4. Only after 30-day resilience test: consider expanding to a second platform.

"Failure by suffocation" = getting traction → adding platforms too early → fracturing focus → killing the golden goose.

### 9. Niche Selection (4-Question Framework)

Answer in order:
1. Trying to make money? No → pick your most interesting topic. Done.
2. Already have a product or personal brand niche? Yes → make content in that exact niche. Done.
3. Fewer than 30 videos made? Yes → "learning era." Make 20–30 reps on any topic. Skills transfer to next niche.
4. Own products/services OR brand deals? Own product → pick a space where you have unique insight OR a customer profile you want to serve for a decade. Brand deals → pick a niche where large companies already pay influencers heavily.

### 10. The AI Content System (5-Step Workflow, 15–30 min/video)

- **Step 1 — Ideas (1 hr/week batch):** Sandcastles watch list + Sort Feed. Extract Lego bricks.
- **Step 2 — Research (3–10 min/video):** ChatGPT prompt → "build a report on [TOPIC], break down the most interesting aspects for max shock, add a contrarian take. Assume I know nothing."
- **Step 3 — Script (5–10 min):** Paste topic + angle into Sandcastles. Select hook template from Vault. Select style template. Edit for personal voice.
- **Step 4 — Record (10 min):** Dialed A/V setup ready. Sit down, press record. Or: HeyGen avatar + 11Labs voice clone for fully AI pipeline.
- **Step 5 — Edit:** No full AI automation yet. Either edit manually with simpler formats (30–60 min) or hire a specialist editor.

### 11. Team Structure (Priority Order)

Add in this exact sequence as budget allows:
1. **Sandcastles.ai (~$40/month)** — saves 10–20 hrs immediately. Non-negotiable first spend.
2. **Human editor (~$3K/month)** — best ROI. Saves 2+ hrs/video = 20–40 hrs/month at volume.
3. **Content operator (~$3–4K/month)** — runs idea mining, research, scripting. Detaches founder from ideation entirely.

Do not hire a full-service agency. Their markup costs more than hiring specialists separately and the output is average.

### 12. The SuperUGC Strategy (Echo Effect for Brands)

1. Find 5–20 micro-creators (1K–10K followers) adjacent to your niche.
2. Pay ~$300/month (B2C) or ~$1,000/month (B2B) for 8+ videos/month.
3. Each creator uses derivative or personal account names.
4. Track all videos to affiliate links.
5. For every ~40 dud videos → 1 mega-viral. With 20–50 creators simultaneously, the brand appears to be everywhere.
6. When one video goes viral → take the script/hook → run it as paid ads on a separate account.

Do not run paid ads from the organic account. Paid traffic dilutes audience avatar → algorithm gets confused → organic reach dies.

## Process

### Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Task |
|-----|------|
| Monday | Idea mining (1 hr): Sandcastles watch list, extract Lego bricks, build topic queue |
| Tue–Fri | 1 short-form video/day: 10 min research → 10 min script → record → send to editor |
| Wednesday | YouTube video script (if applicable) |
| Friday | Review analytics: which hook styles, angles, visual formats performed? |
| Sunday | Update system instructions in Claude Projects based on script edits |

### Per-Video Checklist

- [ ] Idea belongs to a single topic cluster (not shotgun topics)
- [ ] Chose Entertainment OR Education lane (not both)
- [ ] Topic + Premise + Hook defined before scripting
- [ ] Hook has all four components: visual, spoken, text, audio — aligned
- [ ] Script has a rehook at 20–25 seconds
- [ ] Every sentence has only one possible comprehension
- [ ] Key visual B-roll (video clip, not just image) identified for main concept
- [ ] Quality self-rated 7/10 or above before publishing

### 8-Step Playbook (Master Framework)

1. Define your Ideal Viewer Avatar (IVA): demographics, psychographics, platforms, pain points.
2. Find 10–20 winning creators serving your IVA. Use fresh algorithm account + Sort Feed.
3. Find outlier videos: at least 10x the channel's average views.
4. Analyze across all four buckets. Find the biggest opportunity gap.
5. Develop your version: find the white space in idea, hook, story, or visual.
6. Script for comprehension: one possible meaning per sentence.
7. Edit for visual clarity: show the best possible visual paired with every spoken line.
8. Post and repeat daily or weekly for 6–12 months. 200 short-form reps OR 50 YouTube reps before things really compound.

## Anti-Patterns

**The Edutainment Trap** — Making "kind of entertaining, kind of educational" content. Loses both games. Pick a lane.

**Just-In-Time Ideation** — Scrolling the feed daily for ideas. Maximum noise, minimum signal. Hours lost to competitor envy. Batch ideas weekly instead.

**The Risky Rep** — Generating ideas purely from your own head with zero external validation. Zero signal on why anything works.

**Shotgun Topics** — Covering multiple unrelated topics on one channel. Algorithm can't cluster you → less push → growth feels like quicksand. One topic per channel. Two topics = two channels.

**Failure by Suffocation** — Getting traction → immediately expanding to more platforms → fracturing all focus.

**Running Paid Ads on the Organic Account** — Dilutes audience avatar → algorithm confusion → permanent organic reach drop. Use a separate ads account.

**Focusing on Metrics in Season 2** — Tying happiness to views/followers at reps 4–50. Primary reason most people quit. Focus only on skill inputs.

**The Quality Trap** — 8 hours on one video instead of 4 hours × 2 videos. Too much risk on too few reps.

**Over-Stuffing After the Hook** — Dumping details immediately after the hook fires. Find the shortest path to interesting after the curiosity question pops.

**The "You Are the Niche" Strategy** — Combining all your interests on one channel. "Somewhat interesting to a lot of people but super valuable to no one."

**Missing Cheat Code Loop** — Believing a shortcut exists → endless research and course-watching → procrastination disguised as strategy. "There is no cheat code. The reason you watch instead of make is watching is fun and making is work."

**Hiring an Agency Before Specialists** — Pays a markup for average output. Hire editor + content operator + AI tools directly.

**The Boredom of Winning** — Getting initial escape velocity → chasing a new shiny object. "Once you get the machine working, leave it alone."
